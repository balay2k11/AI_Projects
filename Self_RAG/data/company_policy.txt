Company Refund Policy

1. Refund Eligibility
Customers can request a refund within 7 days of purchase.
The product must not be damaged or used extensively.

2. Refund Process
Customers must email support@company.com with their order ID.
Refunds are processed within 5 business days after approval.

3. Late Refunds
If a refund is not received within 10 business days, customers should contact the support team.

4. Exceptions
Digital products are non-refundable once downloaded.
Custom-made products are not eligible for refunds.

5. Support Hours
Customer support operates Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM IST.
